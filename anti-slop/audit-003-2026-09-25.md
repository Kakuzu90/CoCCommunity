# antislop audit 003: public profile (`/u/{username}`)

Scope: `resources/views/pages/profile.blade.php`, `resources/css/profile.css`, `x-ui.stat-block`,
`x-player.verified-badge`, and the PlayerCard as rendered on the profile. The check was run in the
browser at 1024, 768 and about 350px wide. Contrast was computed with `contrast-check.py`.
Nothing here has been changed yet.

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH | R-03 | Hero PlayerCard stats overflow on narrow phones. At about 350px, "1,543" (war stars) is wider than its box, because the hero variant uses `--text-stat` in three fixed columns. |
| 2 | HIGH | R-25 | The TH 18 label on the PlayerCard (tier 7) is #FF4D4D on #3C331A at 3.85:1. That fails 4.5:1 for 16px bold text. It is inside the PlayerCard, but the profile's hero slot is where people see it. |
| 3 | MEDIUM | R-03, R-05 | Owner view between 720 and 1024px: the cover band uses three columns. The Edit profile and Privacy settings buttons take about 260px and leave the name and bio column at about 268px, so the bio wraps at roughly 35 characters. |
| 4 | MEDIUM | C-3, R-20 | Two stat rows sit on top of each other: the hero card's trophies, war stars and XP, then the profile's bases, likes, copies and total war stars. They are styled almost the same, so there are two focal points. The war-star figure also appears twice with different values (1,543 on the card and 3,106 in the total), which reads as a contradiction. |
| 5 | MEDIUM | C-3, R-17 | Three of the four profile stats (bases, likes, copies) are 0 for every user because publishing does not exist until Phase 3. The numbers are real, but a row of zeros for a feature nobody can use yet is filler. |
| 6 | MEDIUM | R-04 | The star icon means two things on one screen: the purple Featured star on the card and the gold war-stars icon in the stat row. The `document` icon for "Base copies" has a weak link to its label. |
| 7 | MEDIUM | R-01, R-31 | The featured hero card's top edge is red (tier 7 uses `--state-danger`). On the page's most prominent element, red reads as an error or warning. |
| 8 | LOW | Part 3 | The cover band for other viewers is a flat box with empty space on the right: no identity motif, and the lowest energy element above the fold. |
| 9 | LOW | C-3 | The Bases tab holds one sentence. Two tabs, one of them empty, add a click for no content until Phase 3. |
| 10 | LOW | Screen reader | The header avatar announces "{name}, verified" and the h1 repeats the name right after, so the name is read twice. |
| 11 | LOW | Deep linking | The selected tab is not in the URL, so a link to someone's Bases tab is not possible. |
| 12 | LOW | Unverified | Once, on first load, the tabs did not switch panels. Could not reproduce after reloading. |

Outside the profile (app shell, seen during the review): the guest top bar is wider than the
screen at 768px (`.app-topbar-actions` reaches x=900), which makes every guest page scroll sideways
at tablet width.

What passed:
- Text contrast: secondary text 9.41:1, muted 5.61:1, gold on surface 9.54:1, delta green 12.16:1, delta red 7.39:1.
- The verified badge is keyboard-focusable, shows its focus outline, and opens its tooltip.
- Tabs have 50px targets and arrow-key navigation.
- No horizontal overflow inside the profile at 320 to 1024px.
- Count-up respects reduced motion.

## Follow-up: all findings fixed (same day)

| # | Fix | Verified |
|---|---|---|

| 5 | Base stats appear only when `features.base_publishing` is on (Phase 3). | Test with the flag on and off. |
| 6 | Icons removed from the profile stat row. | Nothing left to clash with the Featured star. |
| 7 | The top-tier card edge is gold (the spec's "gold ring"); red stays on the TH numeral only. | Checked in the browser: the edge is #F5B800. |
| 8 | The cover has a verified summary: highest Town Hall in the ThBadge hexagon plus the counts. It repeats the PlayerCard motif above the fold. | Added to the gallery. |
